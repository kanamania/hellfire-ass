Link WhatsApp to HellFire

On your phone, open WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device. Scan the QR on this separate screen.

This uses an unofficial linked session. Your server can read captured messages. This version captures text and media metadata; it cannot send messages.

Use the token configured on your relay. It stays in this page’s memory until you clear it or close the page.

Refresh if the QR expires. To revoke access, log out this session in WhatsApp’s Linked devices settings.
